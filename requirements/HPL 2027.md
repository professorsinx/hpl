#### **Why HPL?** 

HPL brings doctors, nurses, technicians, administrators and support staff from different hospitals onto one field, united for the common cause of cancer care: awareness, early detection and compassionate treatment. Every February, in observance of World Cancer Day (4 Feb). 

#### **At a Glance** 

**FORMAT** 10 overs a side, tennis ball **<mark>TEAM</mark>** <mark>11 +2 players, captain and vice-captain</mark> **<mark>STAGES</mark>** <mark>League, semi-finals, final</mark> **<mark>DATES</mark>** <mark>Commencing Feb 4 ( International Cancer Day)</mark> **<mark>VENUE</mark>** <mark>Trichy ( Ground yet to be decided )</mark> **<mark>ENTRY FEE</mark>** <mark>10000 per team</mark> 

#### **<mark>Who Can Play?</mark>** 

Every employee of a private hospital or healthcare institution, permanent or contractual: doctors, nurses, pharmacists, lab and radiology technicians, physiotherapists, paramedics, admin, billing and reception, housekeeping, security and support staff. Players must carry hospital ID and represent only their own hospital. It is a mixed gender match to foster gender equality and gender neutrality.
